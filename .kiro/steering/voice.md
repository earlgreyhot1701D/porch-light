# Voice — copy rules, load-bearing

- Plain language first, official term adjacent: "New material added (Official term: Supplemental packet)". Never remove the official term — the user needs it to call the clerk.
- Never make someone feel late or stupid. Deadlines stated, never scolded. Calm is a feature: "Nothing new for you this week" is a complete, good answer.
- Absence is exactly: **"not located at [url] as of [timestamp]"** — never "missing/overdue/failed".
- Deadlines render in **city local time, always labeled** ("5:00 PM · Pacific, city local time"), never silently converted to the viewer's zone. Relative phrases computed against city time.
- The reserved deadline color (`--deadline` amber) appears ONLY on an approaching comment deadline the user can still act on. Nowhere else, ever. Status is never carried by color alone: shape + word + color, and meaning must survive greyscale.
- Every user-facing string exists in English and Spanish, equal weight, correct `lang` attributes. English can be gender-neutral by accident; Spanish cannot — check every second-person and role noun. Spanish greeting is provisionally "Buenas tardes, vecindad." pending fluent review.
- The greeting "Good afternoon, neighbor." appears once, top of page, lowercase n.
- Verbatim, do not "improve": **"Your list stays on your device. We use it to answer, and never store it."** / "Drafts are yours to finish and send." / "Porch Light never writes your opinion and cannot send anything."
- That first string was changed on Aug 22 (§26c). The old wording, "This list lives on your device. We never see it," became untrue once the watcher began matching live from a transmitted watchlist. Weaker sounding, actually true. Do not revert it, and do not let anything else in the product claim we never see the list.
- No em dashes. No AI cliches (delve, landscape, straightforward, genuinely, honestly). Honest over optimistic; limitations language is a feature.
